## Description

Desktop World Clock Widget puts real clocks on your desktop, not in a window you have to
go and find. Add a card for every city you care about, park them where you want, and they
just sit there and keep time.

**Any city, correct time**

Pick from a curated city list or choose from every IANA time zone, so nothing is out of
reach. Each widget shows the city, the time, the date and the UTC offset.

**Summer time, handled**

Daylight saving rules are built into the app, so the clock and the offset change over by
themselves on the transition day. Nothing to press, and no internet needed: the rules ship
with the app rather than being fetched. Half-hour and 45-minute offsets, southern hemisphere
switches and cities that opted out of daylight saving are all handled correctly.

**Make it yours**

- Choose the font, the size and the text colour
- Pick a background colour, or **use your own picture** as the background
- Adjust opacity to sit lightly over your wallpaper
- Drag a corner to resize, drag the card to move it
- Pin a widget so it stays above your browser and everything else
- Name each widget whatever you want

**Stays out of the way**

The app lives in the notification area, not the taskbar. Widgets appear on the desktop and
their controls stay hidden until you hover. Hide a widget without deleting it, and set it to
start with Windows if you want your clocks back after every reboot.

**Private by design**

No account, no advertising, no analytics, and no network connections at all. Your settings
and any background picture you choose stay on your PC.
