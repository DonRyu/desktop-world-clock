# Privacy Policy — Clock Widget

Last updated: 19 August 2026

## Summary

Clock Widget collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and it
never connects to the internet.

## Data we collect

None. The app does not gather, transmit, or sell any personal information.

## Network use

Clock Widget makes no network requests of any kind. Time zone and daylight saving rules are
bundled inside the application, so the clock stays correct offline. The app reads the clock
from your own PC and nothing leaves your device.

## Data stored on your device

Two things are written to your local application data folder, and nowhere else:

- **Your settings** — the cities you added, fonts, colours, sizes and window positions.
- **Background images you choose** — the picture file is copied into the app's own folder so
  the widget can display it. It is never uploaded.

Nothing in either of those is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or deleting its
application data folder, removes all of it.

## Third parties

No third-party services, SDKs, or trackers are included. The pin and eye icons come from
Bootstrap Icons (MIT licence), which is artwork bundled at build time, not a service the app
talks to.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated text will ship with the version it applies to.
